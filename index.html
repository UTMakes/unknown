Controls & Shortcuts

  • Left Click + Drag on empty space to pan the view
  • Mouse Wheel or +/- buttons to zoom in/out
  • 0 key to reset zoom to 100%
  • Right Click on a node to open upgrade/delete menu
  • Drag from green port to blue port to connect nodes ($10 per cable)

Important Mechanics

  • Connectivity: Nodes turn red when disconnected from Router
  • Heat: Overclocking generates heat. At 100°C, speed drops 30%!
  • Viruses: Random events that infect nodes. Clean for $500 or use Firewalls to prevent
  • Data Flow: Audio → Videos → Images → Files (priority order)
  • Contracts: Complete before time runs out for big rewards!
  • Research Tree: Technologies have prerequisites - plan your path!
